# Palm Bay Social Content

Read `CLAUDE.md`. Every post ties back to something real — a specific lot, a market-brief
item, owner financing, or a local-growth fact. No hype, no invented numbers.

## Produce, per request (default: a batch of 3–5)
For each post:
- **Platform:** IG / FB / LinkedIn (adjust length + tone; LinkedIn = investor/commercial angle).
- **Hook** (first line — stops the scroll).
- **Caption** (2–5 short lines; one concrete fact + one benefit + soft CTA to call 321-333-7230
  or visit the site).
- **Hashtags:** mix local + niche, e.g. `#PalmBayFL #BrevardCounty #SpaceCoast #OwnerFinancing
  #LandForSale #VacantLand #FloridaLand #BuildYourDream`.
- Optional **carousel/reel outline** for how-to or "5 things about owner financing" style.

Content pillars to rotate: (1) featured lot, (2) owner-financing explainer, (3) "why Palm Bay"
local growth, (4) buying-process myth-busting, (5) seller-side ("thinking of selling your lot?").

Write the batch to `content/social/YYYY-MM-DD-social.md` with a simple posting checklist.
These are drafts for human review — never post to any account without explicit go-ahead
and a connected, authorized channel.
