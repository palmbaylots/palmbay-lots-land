📄 OWNER FINANCING PAGE: Representative Example

$25,000 Purchase Price Example

Purchase Price: $25,000
Initial Option Payment (30%): $7,500
Initial Amount Financed: $17,500
Interest Rate: 10% Fixed
Original Term: 120 Months (10 Years)
Initial Monthly Principal & Interest: Approximately $231.26

Step-by-step walkthrough:

  1. Make your initial option payment: You pay $7,500 (30% of $25,000) to secure the lot with an option contract.
  2. Begin monthly payments: Starting the next month, you pay $231.26/month. These payments are applied to principal and interest according to the 10% amortization schedule.
  3. Reach 40% threshold: After approximately 11 months of payments, your total amount paid exceeds 40% of the $25,000 purchase price.
  4. Deed transfers at closing: The transaction closes and the deed transfers to your name.
  5. Financing charge disclosed: A financing charge equal to 10% of your remaining principal balance (approximately $1,652) is disclosed and becomes due. You choose how to handle it.

Two options for the financing charge (approximately $1,652):

✅ OPTION 1: Pay the Financing Charge at Closing

Pay approximately $1,652 at closing.

Your payments: Your remaining principal stays at approximately $16,520. You continue making approximately $231.26/month for approximately 109 months remaining in your original 10-year term.

💰 OPTION 2: Finance the Financing Charge

Add approximately $1,652 to your remaining $16,520 balance = approximately $18,172.

Your payments: Your new balance is re-amortized at 10% fixed interest over approximately 109 months remaining. Your new monthly payment becomes approximately $254.39.

📋 Financing Charge & APR Information

A financing charge equal to 10% of the remaining principal balance applies when the property proceeds to closing.

In this $25,000 representative example, the remaining principal balance at the 40% threshold is approximately $16,520, resulting in an approximate financing charge of $1,652. You may pay this charge at closing or finance it as part of your remaining balance.

APR: The Annual Percentage Rate (APR) reflects the interest rate plus applicable financing charges and may vary depending on the specific transaction and whether the financing charge is paid at closing or financed. Complete financing terms and APR will be provided for your specific purchase.
