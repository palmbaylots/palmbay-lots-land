# Palm Bay Market Brief — 2026-07-25

## Top 3 opportunities (act this week)

1. **NW Palm Bay builder surge + Everlands West approval — position our NW-quadrant lots as the owner-financed alternative to $300k+ new construction.**
   Palm Bay City Council approved the "Palm Vista Everlands West" development — 2,300 homes (1,600 single-family + 760 multifamily) on ~1,100–1,200 acres west of St. Johns Heritage Parkway in NW Palm Bay, plus 145,000 sq ft of commercial and 300+ acres of preserved wetlands; buildout phased through 2036. This lands on top of an already-hot corridor: Lennar and NVR (Ryan Homes) posted $90M in home sales and 234 closings across five subdivisions along the SJHP corridor in the last 12 months (Mar 2025–Mar 2026), with Lennar's entry-level Riverwood product starting at $304,990. Residents are on record worried the still-two-lane Parkway can't absorb it. **Why it matters to us:** every one of those builder sales is a household that could have bought raw land from us instead — our NW-quadrant lots ($40–70k per CLAUDE.md) with 25%/35%-down owner financing are the plainly cheaper entry point, and rising builder activity is upward pressure on nearby raw-land comps. **Action:** pull our current NW-quadrant inventory this week, build a simple "$304,990 finished home vs. $40–70k owner-financed lot" comparison for the blog/social, and push it while Everlands West is still fresh news (approval reported July 13, 2026). 2. **Ashton Park's wastewater backbone expansion along the SJHP corridor — check which of our southern units are in the service-extension path before comps catch up.**
